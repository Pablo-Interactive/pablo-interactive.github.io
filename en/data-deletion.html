Data Deletion

Effective date: October 15, 2026 Last updated: October 15, 2026

On this page

  1. Delete game data on your device
  2. Reset or delete your advertising ID
  3. Request deletion from us
  4. What is deleted and what is kept
  5. Questions

Applies to: Block Rush and all other games published by Pablo Interactive on Google Play. Our games do not use player accounts, so there is no account to close. This page explains how to delete the data related to you.

01Delete game data on your device

All of your game progress (scores, coins, themes, costumes and settings) is stored only on your device. You can delete it yourself at any time:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device and go to Apps.
  2. Select the game (for example Block Rush).
  3. Tap Storage & cache, then Clear storage (or Clear data). Uninstalling the game also removes this data.

Deleting game data is permanent. Your progress, coins and unlocked items cannot be recovered afterwards. Purchases made through Google Play that support restoring can be restored with the same Google account.

02Reset or delete your advertising ID

Advertising data is linked to your device's advertising ID, not to your name. To break the link with past advertising data:

  1. Open Settings and go to Google > Ads (on some devices: Privacy > Ads).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

Google's handling of advertising data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

03Request deletion from us

To ask us to delete any personal data we hold about you, such as emails you sent us, or to ask us to submit a deletion request to our service providers on your behalf:

  1. Send an email to pablointeractive01@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include the game name and, if relevant, the email address you used to contact us. If your request concerns advertising data, you may include your advertising ID (shown in your device's Ads settings).
  3. We will confirm receipt and complete the request within 30 days.

04What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Game progress, coins, items, settingsDeleted when you clear app data or uninstall. We hold no copy.
Your emails and support historyDeleted within 30 days of your request.
Advertising data held by Google AdMobDisconnected from you when you reset or delete your advertising ID; retained by Google as described in its policy.
Purchase recordsKept by Google Play as required for tax, accounting and legal purposes. We may keep minimal records where the law requires.

05Questions

For any question about your data, contact pablointeractive01@gmail.com. More details are in our Privacy Policy.

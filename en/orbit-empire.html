Orbit Empire

Coming soon Android Free Portrait

From a small launch pad to an empire across the Solar System. Build stations, upgrade them, hire managers and keep production running even while you are away. Every chapter brings a new world, new rockets and bigger goals.

Google Play: coming soon Privacy details
Features

What's in orbit

50 stations, 5 worlds

Start on Earth, then build colonies on the Moon, Mars, Mercury and Venus, each station with its own artwork.

Managers

Hire managers for your stations, trigger their special skills at the right moment and multiply production.

Rockets and cosmetics

Customize your rockets and colony, and unlock new rocket hulls and cosmetics.

Your language

Play in 8 languages, including English and Turkish.

Fair by design

Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to make progress.

Research and mini-games

Speed up with dozens of research upgrades and earn extra rewards in the Lunar Landing, Solar Salvage and Signal Memory mini-games.

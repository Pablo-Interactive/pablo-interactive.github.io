Summary. Our games do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email or location. Game progress is stored on your device. Our games show ads through Google AdMob, which collects certain device information, including your advertising ID, to deliver and measure ads. You can control ad personalization at any time. Questions: pablointeractive01@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how Pablo Interactive ("Pablo Interactive", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use our mobile games, including Block Rush (together, the "Games"), and our website at https://pablo-interactive.github.io (the "Website"). Together these are the "Services". By using the Services you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

01Who we are

Pablo Interactive is an independent game developer and is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this Policy. You can reach us for any privacy matter at pablointeractive01@gmail.com.

02Information we collect

a) Information you give us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any attachments or details you choose to include (for example your device model or screenshots).

b) Information stored only on your device

Our Games save your progress locally on your device: scores and high scores, levels, in-game coin balance, unlocked themes and costumes, language and sound settings. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers. We do not operate our own game servers or user accounts.

c) Information collected automatically by third-party services

Our Games use trusted third-party services that may automatically collect information from your device:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, app version, and approximate country derived from your IP address.
  • Identifiers: the Android Advertising ID (or an app-set ID) and IP address.
  • Usage and ad interaction data: ads shown and clicked, rewarded ads completed, and general app interaction events.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data that help keep the Games stable.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details.

04How we use information

We and our service providers use information to:

  • provide the Games, save your progress and remember your settings;
  • show advertising, including rewarded ads that let you continue a run, and measure ad performance;
  • process in-app purchases and restore purchased features;
  • detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse and security issues;
  • diagnose crashes and improve performance, features and balance;
  • answer your support requests and other messages;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

05Legal bases for processing

Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: providing the Games you choose to use and the purchases you make.
  • Consent: personalized advertising and related access to your device's identifiers where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalized advertising, security, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics and improving the Games, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information by law.

06Advertising and your choices

Our Games are free to play and are supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID and device information to show ads that are more relevant to you (personalized ads) or ads based only on general context (non-personalized ads).

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the privacy option in the game's settings, where available.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: on Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You are never required to watch an ad to keep playing normally.

To learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

08When we share information

Besides the service providers listed above, we may disclose information only:

  • if required by law, court order or a lawful request from public authorities;
  • to protect the rights, safety or property of Pablo Interactive, our players or others;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Policy will continue to apply or you will be notified of changes;
  • with your explicit permission.

09Data retention

  • On-device game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game.
  • Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Data held by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. For example, Google retains advertising data as described in its privacy policy.

10Children's privacy

Our Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us at pablointeractive01@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under GDPR and KVKK, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and learn how it is processed;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier lawful processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

To exercise these rights, email pablointeractive01@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Because we do not run user accounts, we may ask for information that helps us identify data related to you, such as your advertising ID or the email address you used to contact us. See also our Data Deletion page.

12International data transfers

Our service providers may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the explicit consent mechanisms provided by applicable law.

13Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, and our providers encrypt data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14Our website

The Website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts and has no sign-up or contact form. When you visit, GitHub may log your IP address for security purposes, and your browser connects to Google Fonts to load fonts. If you click links to our social media profiles, those platforms' privacy policies apply.

Continued use of the Services after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

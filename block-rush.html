Block Rush

Coming soon Android Free to play Portrait

Place, clear, combo, repeat. Block Rush is a satisfying block puzzle where every move counts. Fit the pieces onto the board, clear full rows and columns, and keep the board open as long as you can.

Features

Why you will keep playing

Pure puzzle flow

Drag and drop blocks, clear lines in both directions and build combo streaks for bigger scores.

Pablo and friends

Our mascot Pablo joins every run. Unlock costumes and dress him up your way.

Themes and shop

Earn coins as you play and spend them on new board themes and cosmetic items.

Your language

Play in multiple languages, including English and Turkish.

Fair by design

Ads are optional and rewarded: watch one to continue a run if you want to. Nothing is pay-to-win.

No account needed

Jump straight in. Your progress is saved on your device, no sign-up required.

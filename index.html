About the studio

A small studio named after a real cat

Pablo Interactive started with a simple idea: build the kind of mobile games we actually enjoy playing every day. Our name and mascot come from Pablo, a very real white tabby who supervises most of our development sessions.

We focus on puzzle and casual games for Android, designed for short sessions and long-term fun. We are building our catalog one carefully polished game at a time, and we share our progress on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
